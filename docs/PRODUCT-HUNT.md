## Current Positioning

**Product name**

Wayfinder AI

**Tagline**

See how your work with AI actually evolved.

**Description**

Wayfinder is a local-first desktop app that turns work with AI into a visual
history of how a project evolved. It groups related work into voyages, keeps
alternative and failed paths visible, and links each waypoint to the original
conversation, activity, and artifacts. The product is designed for AI
collaboration across coding, research, writing, design, and other project work;
the current early-access collectors support Codex and Claude Code. Available
for macOS and Windows, with no account or cloud sync.

## Maker Comment

AI tools are very good at producing the next answer. Their interfaces are less
useful when you need to understand why one approach worked, why another failed,
or which evidence changed the direction of a project.

I built Wayfinder as a standalone local-first desktop app for that missing
layer. It reads Codex and Claude Code session history on your computer, groups
related work into project voyages, keeps abandoned branches visible, and links
each waypoint back to the original conversation, tool activity, and real file
changes.

Wayfinder is designed for any project advanced through AI collaboration,
including research, writing, design, and coding. The current early-access
collectors support Codex and Claude Code. The app is open source, available for
macOS and Windows, and has no Wayfinder account or cloud sync.

**What part of an AI collaboration is hardest to reconstruct a week later?**

The final artifact usually survives. The path behind it often does not: the
first assumption, the discarded direction, the evidence that disproved it, or
the reason a different approach finally worked.

Wayfinder keeps those paths local and reviewable across Codex and Claude Code.
When you return to a project, which missing context costs you the most time:
the decision, the failed attempt, or the evidence that settled it?
