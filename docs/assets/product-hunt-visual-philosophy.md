# Cartographic Evidence

Cartographic Evidence treats every decision as a coordinate and every attempt as a visible route. Space is not a backdrop but an argument: paths separate, converge, and remain legible as a record of thought. The composition should feel meticulously drafted, with the restraint and precision of a master cartographer.

Color carries operational meaning. Deep sea green holds the field; warm sand marks origin and safety; blue, violet, and coral distinguish independent routes, outcomes, and human judgment. Each hue must be painstakingly calibrated so the system reads instantly without becoming decorative.

Scale creates the narrative. A small prompt can open into a wide branch; a single restored waypoint can redirect the whole composition. Rhythm comes from repeated nodes, measured intervals, and long breathing spaces, all resolved with expert-level alignment and countless careful refinements.

Typography behaves like map notation: sparse, exact, and secondary to the visual evidence. Short labels anchor interpretation while lines, nodes, and preserved branches do most of the communication. Every word earns its place, and every baseline reflects deep typographic craft.

The finished work should feel like an artifact from an imaginary discipline devoted to studying collaboration. It is analytical without becoming sterile, expressive without becoming illustrative, and unmistakably the product of patient, master-level execution.
